Travel Guide

Driving Directions From Your Area

How to reach White Stone Spa Solapur Road from every major neighborhood across Vijayapura.

From BLDE Road

Distance: 3.8 km • ~7 mins

Head north on BLDE Road Main Rd toward Solapur Road Highway. Continue straight past Gurudwara Arch; White Stone Spa is on the 1st floor above HP Gas.

From Station Road

Distance: 3.2 km • ~6 mins

Take Station Road Main Road towards Janwada Road junction, merge onto Solapur Road Road. Turn right beside Gurudwara Arch.

From Vijayapura Fort / Old City

Distance: 4.5 km • ~9 mins

Drive past Chaubara Tower toward Mohan Market, proceed along the main bypass north directly to Solapur Road.

From Gandhi Chowk / Chidri

Distance: 5.5 km • ~10 mins

Follow Ashram Road toward Ring Road, take the direct flyover route towards Solapur Road Gurudwara Arch.

From Mohan Market / Udgir Rd

Distance: 2.9 km • ~5 mins

Head north along Udgir-Vijayapura Road straight into Solapur Road commercial zone. Look for HP Gas on your right.

From GNDEC & Janwada Rd

Distance: 1.8 km • ~4 mins

Exit Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College campus, head west onto Solapur Road road. Arrive in under 4 minutes.
